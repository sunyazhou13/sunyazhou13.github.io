## Recommending a Great Mouse Software for Mac

A free and open-source software that makes Windows mice scroll naturally on Mac: [mos](https://mos.caldis.me/)

It makes the mouse wheel scrolling on a Windows mouse as smooth as an Apple trackpad.

[Download here](https://github.com/Caldis/Mos/releases/download/3.3.2/Mos.Versions.3.3.2.dmg)
