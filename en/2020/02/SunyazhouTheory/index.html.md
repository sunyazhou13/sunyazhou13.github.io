# Preface

Due to my weak foundational knowledge, what I've achieved is limited to some facts I've learned and observed, and has not yet been elevated to a theoretical doctrine.

## The Lesser-Known "The Sunyazhou Theory"

For a country to develop, it must start with improving productivity. Technology is so far the only powerful tool humanity has discovered to solve productivity problems.
So I divide productivity into three stages:

1. Liberating productivity  
2.  Improving productivity  
3.  Breaking through productivity  

I call it **`《孙亚洲理论》`** (The Sunyazhou Theory). No authoritative journal has published it yet.

## Verifying the Theory

The objective fact that this theory proves is that developing countries must go through the following stages of development:

1. Small-scale manufacturing of usable goods  
2. Optimizing and improving craftsmanship with assembly-line operations  
3. Being phased out and replaced by new products  

So living in the present, whether in school or at work, __technical personnel have a far greater advantage than ordinary personnel, because it validates my theoretical doctrine—engaging in professions that improve productivity.__

I hope that years from now, when people bring up this principle, they'll think: ah, this is the lesser-known "The Sunyazhou Theory."

# Summary

When going home for the New Year, someone always asks how much I make working in Beijing — that kind of thing. I just briefly expressed my views and, half-jokingly, formed my own theoretical doctrine.
