## Development Summary

![macOS icon](/assets/images/20170113LearningmacOSdevelopmentShortcut/CocoaProgrammingForOSX.avif)

1. Complete all __[raywenderlich](https://www.raywenderlich.com/category/macos)__ macOS development tutorials
2. __[Cocoa Programming for OS X (5th Edition)](https://pan.baidu.com/s/1nuMnePj)__ is a must-read (English edition only, no Chinese translation)
3. __[[Advanced.Mac.OS.X.Programming(3rd.2011)].Mark.Dalrymple. Text Version](https://pan.baidu.com/s/1i493Zpz)__ is a must-read

All of the above must be read through at least once, including those two epub books. Although they are all in English (my English is not great but not bad either), reading them is quite easy.

__Blogs__
__[Lao Tan's Notes](http://www.tanhao.me/)__ — This expert was the tech lead for the Sina cloud disk Mac client. I've read and practiced every single one of his articles. If you're learning macOS development, I recommend carefully going through his blog first.
__[喵神](https://onevcat.com/)__
__[巧神](http://blog.devtang.com/)__
These blogs are essential reading for every iOS & macOS developer.
There are many more blogs out there, too many to list. Just search on GitHub.

Feel free to ask questions and exchange ideas. I've been developing for quite a few years now, but never figured out how the blog thing works. Starting from 2017, I've decided to set a small goal: get my blog going. I welcome all criticism and guidance — I'm all ears. Thank you!
