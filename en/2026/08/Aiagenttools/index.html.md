## Background: Why Focus on "Free Computing Power" for AI Office Agents?

In 2026, the cost of calling AI large language models remains a barrier for individual developers, small and medium-sized teams, and ordinary users. Although model capabilities continue to improve, the token-based pricing model for API calls accumulates significant costs for high-frequency usage scenarios (such as automated office work, code assistance, and document processing).

Meanwhile, the domestic AI office agent market is experiencing an intense "**free computing power war**". Major vendors are offering generous free credits and token quotas to capture users and cultivate usage habits:

- **Tencent WorkBuddy** gives 5,000 credits to new users upon registration, with additional daily check-in rewards — effectively amounting to **tens of millions of free tokens**;
- **Alibaba QwenWork** offers 2,000 credits to new users during the public beta, with 500–2,000 additional credits available daily during the first week;
- **ByteDance TRAE IDE** provides 200 credits daily for free users, with 4,000 credits for new user registration;
- **National Supercomputing Internet** grants every OpenClaw user **10 million tokens** for free;
- **Alibaba QoderWork** (now integrated into QwenWork) previously launched an "AI Productivity Plan" offering **tens of billions of credits** for free.

Some bloggers have even stated outright: "**Freeloading is amazing**: a month of unlimited free token computing power — mainstream IDEs, agents, and lobsters, go wild." GitHub has also seen open-source projects dedicated to "**compiling all free LLM model deals across the internet**".

**It is against this backdrop** that this article systematically compiles product overviews, visit volume rankings, macOS support status, official website links, and daily free credit benefits of mainstream domestic AI office agents, providing a practical reference handbook for readers looking to "freeload" computing power.

## VIII. Key Trend Summary

1. **From "Horse Racing" to Consolidation**  
   Tencent merged QClaw into the WorkBuddy ecosystem; Alibaba consolidated QoderWork, Wukong, and MuleRun into "QwenWork"; ByteDance merged the Feishu team into Doubao.

2. **Desktop Entry Point Battle**  
   PC visit volume carries high value — when users open an office agent, it means they have documents to write, spreadsheets to build, and emails to reply to.

3. **Foundation Model Vendors Going Direct**  
   Zhipu (AutoClaw), MiniMax (MiniMax Agent), Moonshot AI (Kimi Work), and StepFun (StepClaw) have all launched standalone desktop products.

4. **Active Open Source Ecosystem**  
   Open-source projects such as OpenClaw, OpenOcta, CoPaw (AgentScope), UITARSdesktop, and AtomCode have enriched market diversity.

5. **Free Computing Power as a Customer Acquisition Weapon**  
   Major vendors are lowering barriers to entry through generous free credits and token quotas. Individual developers and small-to-medium teams are in a golden window of opportunity for "**freeloading**" computing power.
